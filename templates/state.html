About these numbers

Monthly prices are average revenue per kWh (bills divided by usage) reported to the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Per EIA's reporting rules they include customer charges, energy and demand charges, fuel adjustments, environmental surcharges and franchise fees; sales taxes added separately to your bill may not be included.
